The cabins

Two wooden cabins in a large garden: greenery all around, the quiet of a moshav, and full privacy for the whole family. Each cabin sleeps a couple and 6 children: parents downstairs, children on mattresses in the loft. Each cabin has its own jacuzzi.

  • A large, quiet garden

    Greenery all around, the quiet of a moshav, and full privacy for the whole family.

  • A corner for parents

    A quiet double bedroom downstairs — so you get a moment of rest too.

  • A loft for the children

    A roomy loft with mattresses.

  • Your own jacuzzi

    Every cabin has a jacuzzi of your own — a pampering end to a day of trips.

  • A stocked kitchenette

    A coffee machine, coffee, tea, sugar, milk, and disposable utensils.

Outside: a lawn, hammocks, fruit trees, the family's wine-grape vineyard (walks and photos; the grapes stay on the vine), a barbecue, and a pool on a wooden deck that is open in summer only and shared by both cabins.

House rules

  • Couple + 6 children per cabin. Both cabins together: up to 16.
  • The loft is reached by stairs.
  • Pets are welcome in the yard only — not inside the cabin.
  • Arrive before Shabbat. You can leave the car in the parking by the moshav gate, about a 100-meter walk from the cabins.
  • The cabin has a hot plate (plata), an urn (kumkum), and Shabbat timers. The synagogue is about a 5-minute walk.
  • There is a shelter at the house.

For prices and availability, message us on WhatsApp. Miluim resort vouchers (Cal) are accepted.

Shabbat, kashrut, and practical details

Nov is a religious moshav. Arrive before Shabbat begins.

You can leave the car in the parking by the moshav gate, about a 100-meter walk from the cabins. Many guests do this. It is an easy option for heading out on walks on Shabbat without driving inside the moshav.

The cabin has a hot plate (plata), an urn (kumkum), and Shabbat timers.

The synagogue is about a 5-minute walk.

There is a shelter at the house.

Kashrut

For the sake of kashrut, we leave it to you to bring your own dishes. Basic disposable utensils are waiting in the cabin.
