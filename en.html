Moshav Nov, Golan Heights

Tzadik Katamar Cabins

Two large wooden cabins for a whole family. A separate parents' room downstairs, a loft with mattresses for the children, a private jacuzzi in each cabin, and a garden with fruit trees, a vineyard and hammocks.

Each cabin, comfortably: a couple + 6 children (8 people). Both cabins together: up to 16. About 30 minutes to the Kinneret.

  • Couple + 6 children
  • Religious moshav
  • Miluim voucher

The cabins

Each cabin sleeps a couple and 6 children: parents downstairs, children on mattresses in the loft (up a ladder). The private jacuzzi is also the shower. There is a kitchenette, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, a coffee machine, and a bottle of wine when you arrive.

Outside: a lawn, hammocks, fruit trees, the family's wine-grape vineyard (walks and photos, not picking), a barbecue, and a pool on a wooden deck that is open in summer only and shared by both cabins.

House rules

  • Couple + 6 children per cabin. Both cabins together: up to 16.
  • Pets are allowed in the yard only. No animal may enter the cabin.
  • Arrive before Shabbat. Leave about half an hour after Shabbat ends. Guests who drive on Shabbat park at the moshav entrance, about a 100 meter walk.
  • We do not answer the phone on Shabbat.

Kitchen kashrut, Shabbat hot-plate and urn, the distance to the synagogue, and whether there is a protected room are not listed here. Ask Dan before you book. The full guide is in Hebrew.
