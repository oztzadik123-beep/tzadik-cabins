Shabbat, kashrut, and what is good to know

A peaceful Shabbat

Nov is a religious moshav, and you can feel the quiet. Arrive before Shabbat comes in.

You can leave the car in the parking by the moshav gate, about a 100-meter walk from the cabins — very convenient for anyone who wants to head out on walks on Shabbat without driving inside the moshav. Many guests do this.

A hot plate (plata), an urn (kumkum), and Shabbat timers are waiting in the cabin.

The synagogue is about a 5-minute walk.

Every season has its moment

Every season is open to you. If one of them calls, talk to us about the dates.

Hanukkah, from the night of 4 Dec 2026 through 12 Dec 2026

Candles in the yard, and sufganiyot too if the cart in the moshav is open. In winter, ancient Katzrin lights oil lamps at the oil press, and the olive harvest at Karmei Golan in Nov falls in November and December.

Waterfall season, December to March

After the rain, Nahal El Al and Gamla run strong, and then you come back to the jacuzzi. On a flood-warning day, stay out of the channel. The pool in the yard returns in summer.

If plans change

Write to Dan on WhatsApp. This is how the refund works:

Cancellation within 14 days of booking, if at least 7 business days remain before the stay

Under the Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981, a cancellation fee of 5% of the transaction price or 100 NIS, whichever is lower. The rest is refunded.

Cancellation after 14 days, or fewer than 7 business days before the stay

There is no refund.

War or emergency

The Consumer Protection Law does not set an automatic refund for a security situation, war, or Order 8. In those cases we read each request on its own, and try to reach a fair solution, including a new date or a partial refund depending on the circumstances.
