Your place in the Golan

In the quiet Moshav Nov, two large wooden cabins await you, designed for families (couple + 6). Enjoy complete privacy, a refreshing pool, a private jacuzzi, and views of our wine-grape vineyard. We are a short drive from unique coffee carts, the most beautiful springs in the Golan, and just about 20 minutes from the Sea of Galilee.

Everything you wanted to know.

How many guests fit in each cabin?

Each cabin is planned for a couple + 6 children. A private double bedroom on the entry level, and above it a roomy loft with mattresses. The loft is reached by stairs. Both cabins together — up to 16 guests.

Is the pool open year-round?

The pool is open during the summer months. It is built into a wooden deck and shared by both cabins. Each cabin has its own private jacuzzi.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are welcome in the yard only — not inside the cabin.

What is included?

Air conditioning in every cabin, a coffee machine, coffee, tea, sugar, milk, and disposable utensils, wireless internet, a kitchenette, and a private jacuzzi. Prices and details? Send us a WhatsApp — happy to help.

What about Shabbat and kashrut?

Arrive before Shabbat. You can leave the car in the parking by the moshav gate, about a 100-meter walk from the cabins. The cabin has a hot plate, an urn, and Shabbat timers. For the sake of kashrut, we leave it to you to bring your own dishes. Basic disposable utensils are waiting in the cabin.
